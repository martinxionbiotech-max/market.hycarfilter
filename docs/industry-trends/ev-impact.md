# Impact of Electrification on Filter Demand

!!! note "Source attribution"
    This article is an editor's original compilation. **[Industry Knowledge]** = common industry practices; **[Data Citation]** = citing values identical to [data.hycarfilter](https://data.hycarfilter.com); **[Analytical Judgment]** = the researcher's original judgment (Xiaozhu ✍️). Trend data such as penetration rates is **estimated reference / for reference only**. Backlinks: [Aftermarket vs OEM](aftermarket-vs-oem.md).

## 1. A common misreading: EVs don't need filters

**[Analytical Judgment]** Many people think "electric vehicles have no engine, so they don't need filters". This judgment is **half right and half wrong**:

- **The right half**: battery electric vehicles (BEV) have no internal-combustion engine, so they have **no oil filter, no fuel filter, and no engine intake air filter** — these three categories will decline over the long run with electrification.
- **The wrong half**: EVs **still need a cabin filter**, and EVs' demand for "cabin air quality" is often stronger than fuel vehicles' — because the "quietness" of an EV makes odors, dust, and particulates easier to perceive.

## 2. The differentiated impact of electrification on the seven filter categories

**[Industry Knowledge]** Break down demand by powertrain form (internal combustion ICE / hybrid HEV / range-extender REEV / battery electric BEV):

| Category | ICE | HEV/REEV (with internal-combustion engine) | BEV (battery electric) | Long-term trend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Air filter (engine intake) | Needed | Needed | **None** | Declining long-term |
| Oil filter | Needed | Needed | **None** | Declining long-term |
| Fuel filter | Needed | Needed | **None** | Declining long-term |
| Cabin filter (cabin) | Needed | Needed | **Needed (and more important)** | **Rising long-term** |
| Transmission filter screen | Needed | Needed | Partial (reducer lubrication) | Structural change |
| Wiper blade | Needed | Needed | Needed | Unchanged |
| LED lights | Needed | Needed | Needed | Unchanged |

**[Data Citation]** Among hycarfilter's 7 major categories, the three categories of **cabin filters, wiper blades, and LED lights** are "weakly related to powertrain form" and are "electrification-resilient categories" in the electrification era; oil filters, fuel filters, and engine air filters are "pressure-bearing categories" — the category division is identical in value to [data.hycarfilter](https://data.hycarfilter.com).

## 3. The "counter-intuitive incremental demand" of electrification

**[Analytical Judgment]** Electrification is not purely bearish for the filter industry; there are several **counter-intuitive increments**:

1. **Cabin filter upgrades**: EVs are more sensitive to cabin air quality, so the penetration of HEPA-grade cabin filters and activated-carbon filters rises, and **unit price and margins improve**;
2. **The transition-period dividend of hybrid/range-extender**: HEV/REEV still carry internal-combustion engines, so oil filter/fuel filter/air filter demand **still exists during the transition period (likely more than 10 years)**;
3. **Battery thermal-management-related filtration**: some coolant/oil filtration needs are a new-category opportunity (framework observation, not within this site's product scope).

## 4. Recommendations for buyers and suppliers

**[Analytical Judgment]**

1. **Don't panic-sell oil filters/fuel filters**: the global stock and increment of internal-combustion + hybrid/range-extender vehicles keeps demand for these three categories **considerable in the short-to-medium term (10-year scale)**, only with a downward long-term curve.
2. **Lean into cabin filters ahead of time**: upgrade cabin filters from a "subsidiary category" to a "strategic category" — they are the **only certain-to-rise** filter category in the electrification era, and have high added value (HEPA/activated carbon).
3. **Hedge with a "resilient combination"**: cabin filters + wiper blades + LED lights + transmission filter screens form a category combination resilient to the electrification shock; tilt resources here.
4. **Watch the hybrid/range-extender market**: in China and some emerging markets, hybrid/range-extender penetration is fast, and their "carrying an internal-combustion engine" characteristic has **greatly extended the life** of traditional filters.
